How the match score works

Each role gets a score out of 100 from five parts. Roles only appear if they already pass your filters (category, posting window, base pay rule, location, level), so the score ranks roles that fit; it doesn't decide which ones show up.

  • Job category · up to 30. 30 when the role is in one of your selected job categories.
  • Skills & keywords · up to 25. 4 points for each skill turned on in your profile, or extra keyword from your criteria, that appears in the job title or excerpt.
  • Base pay · up to 20. 20 if base starts at or above your minimum, 12 if the range only reaches it, 6 if no base is stated, 3 if only on-target earnings or an aggregator's estimate is given. Commission and bonus never count as base.
  • Location · up to 15. 15 in your commute area or remote with your home state eligible (12 if eligibility is only likely), 8 remote with no state list, 4 elsewhere.
  • Level · up to 10. 10 above your minimum seniority, 7 at it.

Open any row with + to see its breakdown. The score is computed in your browser from your profile and the posting text; nothing is sent anywhere.
